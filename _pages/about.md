Hello everyone, I am Li yixuan, currently serving as a senior algorithm engineer at NetEase Cloud Music. My focus lies in cutting-edge fields such as visual generation, visual understanding, audio information retrieval, and multimodal representation extraction. Leveraging deep learning technologies, I am committed to creating a more personalized and intelligent music experience for users. In the realm of visual generation, I explore how to use algorithms to generate visual elements that match the musical style, adding more creativity and visual appeal to music creation. Regarding visual understanding, I conduct in-depth analysis of music videos and other content to achieve accurate interpretation and application of visual information. In the field of audio information retrieval, I optimize retrieval algorithms to enable users to find their favorite music works more quickly and accurately. And in multimodal representation extraction, I strive to uncover the correlations and integration among different modal data such as music, text, and images, providing more comprehensive and rich data support for music recommendation and content creation.
In terms of academic research, I have published a paper titled "TrOMR: Transformer-based Polyphonic Optical Music Recognition". If you are interested in my work or research field, please feel free to contact me via email at liyixuan03@corp.netease.com. I look forward to exchanging ideas and making progress together with everyone, promoting the application and development of deep learning in the field of music.
